How to use this page

Look up verses by reference

Type a reference; verses appear as you type. Separate several references with ;

  • Gen. 1:1   Eph. 4:4-6   Rev. 21:2, 9-10   Rom. 6:3-5; 8:4
  • John 1 whole chapter   Gen. 1-2 whole chapters   Jude 9 single-chapter book
  • Gen. 1:4-2:3 across chapters   Gen. 1-2:3 chapter 1 start through 2:3
  • Gen. 1:5-2 (second number smaller) means 1:5 to the end of chapter 2
  • Gen. 1:5-7 with a normal hyphen means verses 5 to 7; with an em dash (—) it means 1:5 through the end of chapter 7

Full names, abbreviations and short forms all work (Romans, Rom., rom). Also: jdg Judges, jud Jude, phm / philm Philemon, Song of Songs / Song of Solomons for S.S.

Search by words

  • light finds every verse containing it; god created needs both words
  • "in the beginning" (quotes) finds the exact phrase
  • Greek text: type Greek letters (accents, breathing marks and capitals are ignored, and ς matches σ). Without a Greek keyboard, type Latin letters and they are read as Greek when nothing matches as typed, e.g. logos finds λογος; use q for θ, c ξ, x χ, y ψ, w ω, h η, f φ
  • A space marks a word edge: cherub also finds cherubim, while cherub (trailing space) finds only the whole word, and cherub (leading space) only words starting with it
  • Put the word in quotes to force a word search, e.g. "mark" or "job", since those are also book names

Click the cog icon beside the ? button (or press \) to open the word-search scope panel and limit word searches to the Old Testament, the New Testament, a section (Law, Gospels, …) or any books you tick. Click a section again to remove it. Inside the panel, move with the arrow keys or H J K L, and press Space or Enter to choose. References are never limited by the scope.

Touch screens

Tap verses to select or deselect them. A bar at the bottom lets you Copy (the selected verses, or all listed if none are selected), Present, and Select all / Clear. In presentation mode, swipe left or right (or use the buttons) to change verse, A− / A+ to resize, Fill to fill the screen, and × to exit.

Mouse

Click a verse to select it, Shift+click to select a range, Ctrl/Cmd+click to add or remove one, double-click to present.

Versions

Choose several text files at once, or use Add version later; each becomes a numbered version. Press 1–9 while on a verse or in presentation mode to switch version, or click a version button at the top (touch: the Ver button in presentation mode). When a lookup returns a single verse, it is shown in the current version on top with the other versions listed below it. Otherwise the same verses stay on screen, with their selection, in the new version. Digits typed in the text box still type normally. Word searches run in the version you’re viewing.

Your text file
